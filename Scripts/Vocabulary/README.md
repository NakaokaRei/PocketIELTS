# General Training vocabulary

Original English definitions and examples for 1,000 words and phrases, authored for Pokelts. Each of the ten TSV files contains 100 entries in the form `expression|definition|example`. Each example must contain its expression (case-insensitive), and expressions must be unique across topics.

Run `python3 Scripts/build-vocabulary.py` from the repository root to validate the sources and regenerate `Resources/Vocabulary/GeneralTrainingWords.json`. The first 40 rows of each topic are Foundation (4–5 guide), the next 40 Developing (5.5–6.5 guide) and the final 20 Advanced (7+ guide). These are editorial study groupings, not IELTS band assignments or an official exam word list.

Topic scope follows everyday and workplace contexts in IELTS General Training Reading and letter-writing contexts in Writing Task 1, with broader social and environmental vocabulary for Reading Section 3 and Writing Task 2. All meanings and examples are newly written; this dataset does not reuse the removed WordNet subset.
